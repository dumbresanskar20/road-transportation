Rule-Based AI Architecture & Documentation

Rule Based Expert System For Road Transportation

A production-grade Artificial Intelligence expert system implementing declarative knowledge engineering, forward-chaining deduction, dynamic rule authoring, transparent explanation facilities, and AI integration.

Problem Statement

Modern road transit networks face unpredictable hazards including sudden weather shifts, heavy vehicular congestion, roadway closures, surface degradation, disabled vehicles, and accidents. Commuters and logistics operators often struggle to synthesize multiple simultaneous road variables into prioritized, safe operational decisions.

This system addresses that challenge by formalizing transport safety heuristics into structured declarative IF–THEN production rules. The forward-chaining inference engine autonomously matches prevailing road facts against the knowledge base, deriving explainable and actionable advisories, augmented by AI for contextual deep-dives.

Core Objectives
  • Dynamic Knowledge Representation:
    Formalize road safety domains into modular JSON production rules with full support for user-defined dynamic rules.
  • Forward-Chaining Inference:
    Execute a deterministic data-driven forward reasoning engine evaluating conjunctions (AND) and disjunctions (OR).
  • Conflict Resolution & Prioritization:
    Enforce safety hierarchies where emergency and road closure rules supersede routine commuting advisories.
  • AI Integration & Explanation (XAI):
    Provide detailed step-by-step logic traces alongside generative AI insights.
Core Artificial Intelligence Concepts
Knowledge Representation

Transportation domain expertise is encoded as declarative production rules with explicit premises, logical operators, and consequents, completely decoupled from procedural code.

Forward Chaining

A data-driven reasoning strategy starting from known user facts (working memory) and driving forward through rule premises to assert valid conclusions and recommendations.

Conflict Resolution

When multiple rules activate concurrently, conflict resolution arbitrates output by priority weighting (High > Medium > Low) and deduplication.

Explanation Facility

Addresses the classic expert system requirement for transparency by logging evaluation traces showing each condition clause evaluated to TRUE or FALSE.

Generative AI Augmentation

AI integration provides deep-dive contextual hazard audits, driver psychology suggestions, and intelligent knowledge base gap evaluations.

Persistent Memory

Every assessment episode is serialized and stored in an ACID-compliant SQLite relational database, enabling longitudinal audit trails and empirical analytics.
